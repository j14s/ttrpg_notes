# Wilbur "Burry" Worthmore III

## TL;DR

Burry drank, whored, and philandered his way through Prohibition(and the first couple years after). Spending his inherited portion of the family fortune like every waking hour was a reason to booze and carouse. That he was thought of as the "black sheep" of that family came as a surprise to no one, including Wilbur himself. When his grusome and mysterious death occurred, his closest family members were mostly surprised that a) he had actually made it to 32 years of and and b) it wasn't at the hand of an angry husband.
