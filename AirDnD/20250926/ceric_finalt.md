# Ceric Finalt

- Fighter
- Stoic
- Hand of Bereth
  - Believes he is the judgement of Bereth and if you should find yourself with Ceric in your path, then he(Ceric) has been put there as judgement from Bereth.
  - It is not vengence on Ceric's part, just metting out a sentence beyond himself. He is quite uninvested most of the time.
  - Life is almost "out of his control" as they are all consequences of events that started rolling a long time ago.
